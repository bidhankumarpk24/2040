About 2048 Master

2048 Master is a modern browser-based puzzle game inspired by the classic 2048 tile-merging challenge. Slide, merge, and build your board to chase a new high score.

✧

How to Play

  1. Use arrow keys or swipe to move the tiles.
  2. Matching tiles merge when they collide.
  3. Every valid move creates a new tile.
  4. Keep merging tiles to reach 2048.
  5. Plan ahead and avoid filling the board.
