About 2048 Master

2048 Master is a modern browser-based puzzle game inspired by the classic 2048 gameplay. Merge matching tiles, plan your moves and try to reach the 2048 tile.

⌘

How to Play

  1. Use arrow keys, WASD, or swipe to move the tiles.
  2. Matching tiles merge when they collide.
  3. Every valid move creates a new tile.
  4. Keep merging tiles to reach 2048.
  5. Plan ahead and avoid filling the board.
